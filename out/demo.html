Demo · Scenario replay

Concierge activation, on demand.

Explore destination comparison, package selection and practical travel questions, then test restraint and dismissal. Each scenario starts a fresh Wanderly session in Rules mode and closes the previous chat window; Adobe conversation history is preserved. Open the inspector in a second tab (it follows the latest session).

Right moment

A visitor switches between Bali and Maldives and opens the comparison without deciding.

Expect: Adobe Brand Concierge opens with a clearly labelled suggestion to compare the current destinations. In default mode the visitor chooses whether to send it; automatic sending is legacy opt-in.

Choose a package

A visitor compares Island Rhythm and Bali Slow Living, revisits their prices, and has not chosen a journey.

Expect: Chat suggests comparing the two Bali packages by nights, pace, inclusions and illustrative price. No question is sent until the visitor chooses to submit it in default mode.

Practical travel questions

A visitor planning Japan checks transfer guidance and returns to the entry-requirements question before selecting dates.

Expect: Chat suggests how to check Japan entry requirements, rather than comparing destinations or packages. The demo cannot determine visa eligibility or guarantee entry.

Right restraint

The same comparison, but the visitor then searches dates and starts booking.

Expect: Booking progress is already present when the agent evaluates, so the chat stays closed and no question is suggested or sent.

Respectful adaptation

The same comparison, but the visitor closes the chat.

Expect: Close the chat using its side icon. Keep browsing: the same assistance is not triggered again.
